Add it to Outlook

Pick the version of Outlook you use. If you use Outlook on more than one computer, add your signature on each one.

  1. Make sure New Outlook is switched on. Look for the New Outlook switch at the top right of Outlook, or under the Outlook menu. The older version of Outlook for Mac breaks the signature images.
  2. Click Copy signature.
  3. In Outlook, go to Outlook > Settings > Signatures.
  4. Click + to add a new signature and call it Ka Uruora.
  5. Click into the empty signature box and paste with ⌘ Cmd + V.
  6. Set it as your default for new messages, then save and close the window.
New Outlook
  1. Click Copy signature.
  2. In Outlook, click the Settings cog at the top right, then go to Account > Signatures.
  3. Click New signature and call it Ka Uruora.
  4. Click into the signature box and paste with Ctrl + V.
  5. Choose it as your default for new messages, then click Save.
Classic Outlook
  1. Click Copy signature.
  2. In Outlook, go to File > Options > Mail > Signatures.
  3. Click New and call it Ka Uruora.
  4. Click into the Edit signature box and paste with Ctrl + V.
  5. Under Choose default signature, pick it for new messages, then click OK.
  1. Click Copy signature.
  2. Open Outlook in your browser at outlook.office.com.
  3. Click the Settings cog at the top right, then go to Account > Signatures.
  4. Click New signature, call it Ka Uruora, then click into the box and paste.
  5. Choose it as your default for new messages, then click Save.
